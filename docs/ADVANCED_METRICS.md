## Advanced Metrics Calculations

### PEG Ratio (Price/Earnings to Growth)
- **Formula:** PEG = (P/E Ratio) / (EPS Growth Rate)
- **Interpretation:**
  - < 1.0 = Undervalued
  - 1.0-2.0 = Fair value
  - > 2.0 = Overvalued

### Free Cash Flow (FCF)
- **Formula:** FCF = Operating Cash Flow - Capital Expenditures
- **Use:** Measures cash available for expansion, dividends

### Return on Invested Capital (ROIC)
- **Formula:** ROIC = (Net Income - Dividends) / Total Capital
- **Good:** > 15%
- **Excellent:** > 20%

### Altman Z-Score (Bankruptcy Prediction)
- **Formula:** Z = 1.2X1 + 1.4X2 + 3.3X3 + 0.6X4 + 1.0X5
- Where:
  - X1 = Working Capital / Total Assets
  - X2 = Retained Earnings / Total Assets
  - X3 = EBIT / Total Assets
  - X4 = Market Cap / Total Liabilities
  - X5 = Sales / Total Assets
- **Interpretation:**
  - > 2.99 = Safe zone
  - 1.81-2.99 = Grey zone
  - < 1.81 = Distress zone

### Quick Ratio (Acid Test)
- **Formula:** (Current Assets - Inventory) / Current Liabilities
- **Healthy:** > 1.0

### Operating Margin
- **Formula:** Operating Income / Revenue
- **Good:** > 15%

### Asset Turnover
- **Formula:** Revenue / Average Total Assets
- Higher = more efficient asset use
